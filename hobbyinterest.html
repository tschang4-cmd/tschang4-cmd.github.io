My Hobbies & Interests

Reading

For as long as I can remember, I have always loved reading. Reading allows me to use my imagination and experience the world of the story! Listed below is my favorite genre and some book recommendations!

Romance/Young Adult (YA)
- "Better Than the Movies" by Lynn Painter: The story follows a high school senior, Liz, who explores the idea of romantic idealism vs. reality, along with grief and growing up through humorous, heartfelt moments.
- "The Cheat Sheet" by Sarah Adams: The story follows two best friends who develops feelings for each other. This book as the "fake dating" trope, which can be a fun twist when reading novels!
- "The Right Move" by Liz Tomforde: The story explores two very different individuals that fall in love. The book explores themes of trust, vulnerability, change and identity, as well as the balancing of professional vs. personal life. This book is a PERSONAL FAVORITE!
