My College Life

Campus Life

For me, campus life feels like a series of mini-quests that eventually make up my whole routine as a college student. Some mornings, I rush to class because I overslept, while on others, I do homework, grab a quick bite to eat between classes, or meet up with friends to hang out. Because of this, every day is filled with something new to do, and it encourages me to always be on the go.

Extracurriculars

Even though I am always on the go, I also enjoy getting involved with many student organizations at UW-Madison. Based on my personal experience, student organizations have helped me build a community on campus, provided me with resources to help me grow professionally, and supported me in developing leadership skills to bring into my future career. Additionally, student organizations are the best places to make new friends and connect with people on campus! Many of my friends right now were met through my student organizations: Asian American Student Union (AASU), Student Retail Association (SRA), and HMoob American Student Committee (HMASC).

Here are THREE Student Organizations I have taken a part of:

Asian American Student Union (AASU); Student Retail Association(SRA); HMoob American Student Committee (HMASC)
